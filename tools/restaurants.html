    What helps AI assistants recommend you

    1. Claim and complete your Google Business Profile, with hours, menu, and photos.
    2. Keep your name, address, and phone number identical on every site and app.
    3. Collect fresh reviews, and encourage guests to name the dishes they loved.
    4. Put your full menu on your website as text, not only as an image or PDF.
    5. Get mentioned by local food writers, neighborhood guides, and community pages.
    6. Ask your web builder to add restaurant structured data (schema) to your site.
